7 techniques that improve response quality

1. Give an example

Show 1-2 samples of what you like.: "That's how good it sounds.": "Treat the author to coffee." Do it in the same spirit."

2. Ask step by step for difficult tasks

"First, suggest the structure, then write the text." This reduces the number of errors.

3. Specify the audience

"For beginners" and "for experts" will give completely different texts.

4. Set the format limits

"Table of 3 columns", "list of 5 items", "paragraph up to 300 characters".

5. Use negative constraints

"Don't use the words: innovative, unique, exclusive".

6. Iterate, rather than starting over

If the answer is almost good, say, "Option 2 is good, but make it shorter and remove the exclamation mark." It's faster than writing a new promt.

7. Ask the AI to ask you questions

"Before you write, ask me 3 clarifying questions." It often reveals something that you didn't take into account.
